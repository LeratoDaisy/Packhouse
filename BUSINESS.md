# Packhouse Business Case

## Problem

Small online sellers often reach a point where fulfilling orders becomes a second business. They have to keep stock somewhere, find packing materials, prepare parcels, coordinate drivers or couriers, handle failed deliveries and process returns.

The cost is not only delivery fees. Owners lose time on repetitive fulfilment work, make avoidable stock mistakes and struggle to scale order volume without adding operational overhead.

## Customer

### Primary customer
Small and growing e-commerce sellers who already receive online orders but do not have their own fulfilment operation.

### User
The business owner or fulfilment operator who needs to know what must be packed, what has shipped, what stock remains and what has been returned.

## Solution

**Packhouse provides shared fulfilment infrastructure that lets small online businesses outsource the physical work between receiving an order and completing the delivery.**

## Impact

Packhouse should reduce:
- time spent manually preparing orders;
- fulfilment errors;
- stock visibility problems;
- delays caused by fragmented delivery coordination.

Key MVP measures:
- average time from order received to dispatch;
- orders fulfilled without an exception;
- stock discrepancies;
- return processing time.

## Revenue

Packhouse can charge:
- per-order fulfilment fee;
- monthly storage fee based on inventory volume;
- delivery coordination fee;
- returns processing fee.

## Go-to-market

Start with a small group of local online sellers already selling through Instagram, WhatsApp, Shopify or similar channels. Acquire the first customers through direct outreach, seller communities, referrals and partnerships with packaging/delivery providers.

## Competition

Traditional courier companies primarily move parcels. Self-fulfilment requires sellers to do the operational work themselves. Packhouse sits between those models by providing the fulfilment operation itself: receiving, storing, picking, packing, dispatching and returns.

## Why now

The opportunity is created by the growth of small online sellers and the operational burden that appears as their order volume grows. Packhouse focuses on the part of e-commerce that is often invisible to the customer but expensive for the seller: fulfilment.
